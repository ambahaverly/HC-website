Advisory Partnership

Flexible, on-demand housing finance expertise for local authorities, through one annual subscription.

Expert housing finance advice, when you need it.

Managing housing finance in a local authority has never been more complex. From the pressures of the Housing Revenue Account to the growing cost of temporary accommodation and the governance demands of housing companies, finance teams are being asked to do more with less.

The Advisory Partnership gives your council direct, on-demand access to specialist housing finance expertise, without the cost and commitment of a full-time hire. Through a simple annual subscription, you get unlimited access to our consultants across the areas that matter most.

What the partnership covers

  • Housing Revenue AccountBusiness planning, rent setting, self-financing, capital financing strategies, headroom analysis and 30-year plan modelling.
  • Homelessness and temporary accommodationGeneral Fund cost pressures, acquisition and funding strategies, and budget management.
  • Local authority housing companiesGovernance, financial modelling, dividend strategies, and compliance with IFRS 16 and other accounting standards.

Members can call, email or meet our team as often as needed. There are no per-query charges, no retainer limits and no surprise invoices: just consistent, expert support throughout the year.

Why councils choose a subscription

  • Cost-effective. A fraction of the cost of recruiting or retaining a specialist in-house.
  • Immediate access. No procurement delays, no scoping exercises, no waiting for a project to be approved.
  • Broad expertise. Our team has worked across district, borough, county and unitary authorities.
  • Continuity. Your account is supported by the same consultants, who build deep knowledge of your organisation over time.
  • Flexible. Scale up to a higher tier or commission additional project work at any time.

Frequently asked questions

How is the Advisory Partnership different from commissioning a project?

A project engagement is scoped and time-limited. The Advisory Partnership is an ongoing relationship: your team can ask questions, sense-check decisions or request analysis at any point during the year, without raising a purchase order for each interaction.

Is there a minimum contract period?

Subscriptions are typically annual. We can discuss shorter arrangements depending on your circumstances. Contact us to find out what works best for your authority.

Can we add project support during the subscription?

Yes. Many members also commission us for larger pieces of work such as HRA business plan reviews, housing company establishment or financial appraisals. These are charged separately, but members benefit from priority scheduling and preferential rates.

Which tier of local authority is this suitable for?

We work with district and borough councils, unitary authorities and London boroughs. If your authority holds a Housing Revenue Account, manages temporary accommodation or is exploring or operating a housing company, the Advisory Partnership will add value.
