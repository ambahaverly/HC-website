Project Management

Delivering complex public sector programmes on time and on budget.

Public sector projects face a unique set of challenges.

They are highly scrutinised, must navigate complex governance, often depend on government funding with strict compliance requirements, and are delivered by teams that are already stretched.

We bring the structure, discipline and experience needed to keep programmes on track, whether you need a business case drafted, a funded capital programme managed or a transformation project overseen from start to finish.

What we deliver

  • Business casesOutline and Full Business Cases to HM Treasury Green Book and funding body requirements.
  • Funded capital projectsCompliance reporting, grant drawdown management and funder liaison.
  • Peer review and challengeIndependent review of project plans, risk registers and governance arrangements.
  • Budget and year-endFinancial planning and close-down support for complex programmes.
  • Programme governancePMO functions, reporting frameworks and escalation procedures.
  • Stakeholder managementEngagement with elected members, government departments, partners and communities.

Our track record

Our team has delivered project management across housing development, regeneration, service transformation and commercial activity within local government. We understand the political and organisational dynamics of public sector delivery and how to navigate them.

Frequently asked questions

Can you provide project management on an interim basis?

Yes. We regularly provide project managers and programme directors on an interim basis to cover capacity gaps, provide specialist expertise or support a specific phase of delivery. Engagements range from a few weeks to over a year.

Do you work with Homes England or MHCLG-funded programmes?

Yes. We have managed projects funded through Homes England, MHCLG and other government bodies, and understand the monitoring, reporting and compliance requirements involved.

Can you help us write a business case for a housing scheme?

Yes. We regularly produce Outline and Full Business Cases for housing development and regeneration projects, including those needing Green Book compliance, working closely with your housing, finance and legal teams.
