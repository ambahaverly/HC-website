Buying through a framework

Public sector buyers can commission us through any of these routes, by direct award or mini-competition depending on the framework rules, without running a full tender of their own.

YPO 1141
Certified to supply through Consultancy+, sole provider of YPO framework 1141 for Managing Consultancy and Professional Services. Supports direct award, typically within five to seven days, or a mini-competition. Framework details
NEPRO³
Accredited supplier on NEPRO³, Bloom's compliant route to market for public sector professional services, with fast mini-competitions. Framework details
NHS LPP DPS
Estates and Facilities Consultancy Services Dynamic Purchasing System (ref LPP/2020/015), open to all public sector bodies until June 2028 through further competition. Approved for Lot 5 (Construction Project Management Services), Lot 9 (Estates Strategy and Business Case Development Services) and Lot 11 (Property Acquisition, Disposal and Management Advisory Services).
