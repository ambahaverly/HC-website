Training and Development

Expert-led housing finance training for local authority teams.

The legislative, regulatory and financial landscape for council housing never stands still.

New legislation, revised accounting standards, changing government policy and cost-of-living pressures all place new demands on housing finance professionals.

Our trainers are active practitioners who bring real-world experience from working across dozens of councils, not just theory. Every session is practical, accessible and directly applicable to participants' day-to-day roles.

Formats

  • Regional seminarsFull and half-day events bringing together finance professionals from several authorities.
  • In-house trainingBespoke sessions on-site or online, tailored to your authority and team.
  • WebinarsShorter, focused sessions, live or on demand.

Topics

  • Housing Revenue Account: fundamentals, business planning, rent setting and self-financing
  • Minimum Revenue Provision: policy options, accounting treatment and recent case law
  • Temporary accommodation: financial management, cost pressures and funding options
  • Housing companies: establishing, governing and accounting for wholly-owned companies
  • Capital financing: borrowing, the Prudential Code and treasury management basics
  • Housing development finance: development appraisals, viability and grant funding
  • Financial resilience: budget management under pressure, reserves strategy and medium-term planning

If you have a topic or team need that isn't listed, get in touch. We regularly develop bespoke training in response to client requests.

Frequently asked questions

How do we arrange in-house training?

Tell us your team's size, current knowledge level and the topics you are most interested in. We will design a session for you and provide a quote. Most in-house sessions can be arranged within four to six weeks.

Are webinars available after the live event?

Yes. Recordings are made available to registered participants afterwards. Ask us about on-demand access when you enquire.

Do you offer discounts for multiple bookings or consortia?

Yes. We work with regional finance networks and consortia to deliver cost-effective shared training, and are happy to coordinate sessions with neighbouring authorities.
