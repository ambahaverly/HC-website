Financial Appraisals

Rigorous, independent investment appraisals to support defensible public sector decisions.

Every significant decision a local authority makes carries financial risk.

Whether you are acquiring investment property, commissioning a new housing development, establishing a trading company or allocating capital across a regeneration programme, a robust appraisal makes sure costs are justified, risks are understood and the decision is defensible.

We combine deep knowledge of public sector finance with practical expertise in housing, regeneration and commercial activity to give you a thorough, independent assessment you can rely on.

What we appraise

  • Investment propertyCommercial, residential and mixed-use acquisitions.
  • Housing developmentNew build, regeneration and estate renewal programmes.
  • Housing companiesViability of wholly-owned council housing company business models.
  • Private finance and partnershipsRisk transfer and value for money.
  • Temporary accommodationCost comparison of options and long-term financial modelling.
  • Developer viabilityReview of Section 106 and affordable housing contributions.
  • Business casesOutline and Full Business Cases for capital programmes.

Our approach

We work closely with your finance, housing and legal teams to understand the objectives and constraints of each appraisal. Our outputs are clear, well-structured and designed to support internal decision-making and external scrutiny, including by overview and scrutiny committees, external auditors and government funders.

When we review viability assessments submitted by private developers, our goal is to make sure the affordable housing element of a scheme is maximised and that the council is not accepting less than it is entitled to.

Frequently asked questions

At what stage should we commission a financial appraisal?

As early as possible, before significant resources are committed. An early appraisal can show whether a project is viable in principle and what the key risk factors are. We also provide appraisals at later stages, for example to review assumptions before a final decision or to give independent challenge to an existing business case.

Can you challenge a developer viability assessment?

Yes. We regularly review viability assessments submitted with planning applications, scrutinising the assumptions, identifying where inputs may be optimistic or conservative, and giving the council an independent view of what affordable housing contribution is achievable.

Do your appraisals meet Green Book requirements?

Yes. Our investment appraisals for capital projects follow HM Treasury Green Book guidance, including options appraisal, benefit valuation and sensitivity analysis where required.
