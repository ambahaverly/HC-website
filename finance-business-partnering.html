Finance Business Partnering

Embedding financial expertise at the heart of your public services.

The most effective local authority finance teams don't just report on numbers. They shape strategy, challenge assumptions and enable better decisions.

That is exactly what a skilled Finance Business Partner does. We provide experienced partners who work alongside your service teams, bringing financial rigour to operational decisions, helping managers understand the financial implications of their choices, and supporting your organisation to achieve its strategic objectives.

What our business partners do

  • Act as a trusted financial adviser to service directors and senior managers
  • Analyse and interpret financial data in a clear, accessible way
  • Challenge assumptions and present options to support better decision-making
  • Support budget setting, forecasting and monitoring cycles
  • Help service teams understand their financial position and build financial literacy
  • Identify efficiency opportunities and contribute to transformation programmes
  • Produce business cases, options appraisals and investment proposals
  • Maintain financial stewardship and governance standards

Where we add most value

Our partners are particularly effective in services going through significant change: a restructure, a new delivery model, a capital programme or a response to budget pressure. We bring the external perspective and specialist experience that internal teams often can't easily access.

We have supported housing, homelessness, regeneration, adult social care and commercial services within local authorities, as well as corporate finance functions undertaking transformation.

Frequently asked questions

How is a Finance Business Partner different from a management accountant?

A management accountant typically focuses on reporting, reconciliation and compliance. A Finance Business Partner goes further, actively engaging with service managers to influence decisions, challenge thinking and drive better outcomes. The role is more strategic and forward-looking.

Can you provide interim cover as well as long-term partnerships?

Yes. We regularly provide interim Finance Business Partners to cover vacancies, maternity leave or periods of high demand. We can also support on a project basis where a specific transformation or programme needs dedicated financial input.

How quickly can you mobilise?

We aim to start new engagements within two to four weeks. For urgent requirements, contact us directly to discuss availability.
